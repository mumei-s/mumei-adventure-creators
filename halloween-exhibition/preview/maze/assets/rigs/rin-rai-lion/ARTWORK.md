# ライちゃん / Rai

A childlike golden boy lion cub for `airoumu_rin`. Identity was visually checked against the current [profile avatar](https://note.com/airoumu_rin), and the witch hat, pumpkin badge, cape, plaid shorts, striped socks and black boots follow the [submitted Halloween article](https://note.com/airoumu_rin/n/n7179d2f821e8). The article's unintended female Inori variation is excluded.

## Artwork and animation

- Built-in image generation produced one transparent 1254 × 1254 painterly component sheet, reviewed before integration.
- 52 selected components form four cardinal views. Both arms have independent upper sleeve and forearm/paw parts; both legs have independent thigh and striped-sock/boot parts. The cape and tail have separate secondary motion.
- `authoring.json` records source rectangles, component identifiers, packing scale and exact clipping decisions. Redundant sleeve/cuff pixels in the torso and upper sleeves are excluded so the articulated arms do not double them. Waistband-only shorts overlap the separately articulated thigh/shorts cuffs.
- `parts.webp` uses lossless WebP. The 1024 × 1024 fallback atlas is rendered from this same rig, with four frames for each of front, left, right and back. It is WebP quality 90 with preserved alpha.
- No whole-body wiggle, runtime mirroring or reuse of another participant's artwork.

## Verification

The actual owner-rig Canvas renderer was inspected as four assembled views and a walk/stop contact sheet. Across 1,440 motion samples at 76px and 86px projection, neither foot contact was lost simultaneously, planted feet had zero drift, and the 320 × 384 live composer had no clipped frames. All fallback source rectangles have measured alpha bounds inside their cells.

The runtime provides four cardinal drawings and articulated motion, not independently authored diagonal transition or attack drawings. Browser, physical-device and real WebGL gameplay were not reviewed in this asset task.

## Head attachment correction, 2026-10-24

After rechecking the submitted article header and wizard illustrations at approximately equal cheek width, the four head pivots are lowered by exactly eleven rig pixels (four further pixels beyond the initial seven-pixel adjustment), so the broad mane overlaps the collar rather than joining it only at the narrow fur tip. Head scale, source artwork, torso, limbs, skeleton, motion and output normalization are unchanged. The pumpkin fastening remains visible in the front and profile views. Larger fifteen- and nineteen-pixel candidates were rejected because they obscure the fastening. This adjusts attachment only; it does not claim to reproduce the source artwork’s pose or body volume.

The fallback is rebuilt through the existing `drawOwnerPose` renderer by `scripts/build-rin-rai-atlas.mjs`. With the optional development-only `@napi-rs/canvas` module available to Node, run `node scripts/build-rin-rai-atlas.mjs`, or append `--check` to verify the bytes, hash and measured alpha bounds. No Canvas dependency is required by the game or normal tests.

The recovered original bake uses a 0.83 scale, anchor (128, 238), 256-pixel cells, directions down/left/right/up, idle plus quarter-stride walk samples, stride 0.7 and speed 0.7. Cell-local rendering preserves the original body/leg poses, scale and ground anchors. Rendering every non-head part independently gives byte-identical RGBA across all sixteen frames, and every composite change is inside the old/new head-mask union. The old fixed y >= 160 cutoff is no longer a valid body-only mask: the lowered mane touches two alpha pixels at left-view frame 2, local (109, 160) and (110, 160), each changing by one alpha level. No body pixels outside the head-mask union change. Frame contentHeight remains 219.95, avoiding any enlargement of the torso or legs. The WebP rebuild verifies lossless alpha before saving.

Final native-Canvas checks covered 4,320 walk/stop samples at four cardinal directions and both near-45-degree turn extremes, projected at 76px and 86px. Baseline and corrected skeleton poses remained identical, with zero clipped frames and zero unsupported frames. The head's maximum sampled rotation was 0.03530 radians. Four-direction before/after (published seven-pixel attachment to eleven pixels total), fallback walking frames, maximum-bob poses and turn extremes were visually inspected. These checks do not substitute for physical-device or real WebGL gameplay review.
